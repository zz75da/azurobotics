# Checking the spindle drive power supply

> **Synthetic note.** Written for a personal portfolio project to demonstrate retrieval. It is not a real manufacturer procedure; do not use it on real equipment.

## When to use

A power failure (PWF) occurs in AI4I when the power, torque multiplied by rotational speed in rad/s, is below 3500 W or above 9000 W.

## Procedure

1. Read the drive's power reading during a standard cycle.
2. Compare it with the 3500 W to 9000 W operating band.
3. Check the supply voltage at the drive input terminals with the cabinet open and locked out.
4. Tighten loose terminals and look for burn marks on contactors.
5. Record the measured power for the next comparison.
