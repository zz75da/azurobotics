# Coolant level and pump check

> **Synthetic note.** Written for a personal portfolio project to demonstrate retrieval. It is not a real manufacturer procedure; do not use it on real equipment.

## When to use

A low coolant level or a weak pump reduces heat removal and can lead to heat dissipation failures.

## Procedure

1. Check the coolant level against the minimum mark on the reservoir.
2. Top up with the specified concentration, measured with a refractometer.
3. Listen to the pump at start-up; cavitation noise indicates air in the circuit.
4. Bleed the circuit and check the flow at the nozzle.
5. Clean the coolant filter screen weekly.
