# Cleaning the heat exchanger and fans

> **Synthetic note.** Written for a personal portfolio project to demonstrate retrieval. It is not a real manufacturer procedure; do not use it on real equipment.

## When to use

Poor heat dissipation is often caused by dust on the heat exchanger fins and fan blades.

## Procedure

1. Lock out the machine and let it cool for 20 minutes.
2. Remove the exchanger cover and vacuum the fins from the clean-air side.
3. Wipe the fan blades and check they turn freely without noise.
4. Refit the cover and run the machine for 10 minutes at nominal speed.
5. Confirm the temperature difference has returned above 8.6 K.
